# Clipping for Income Skill

## Overview
Transform long-form videos (streams, podcasts, YouTube) into 15-45s vertical clips for TikTok/Reels/Shorts. Monetize via campaign platforms that pay per 1,000 verified views. No followers required - pay is based on views, not audience size.

## Business Model
- **Input**: Authorized long-form source videos + your editing/hook design
- **Process**: Join platform → apply to campaign → post clips → submit links → get paid per 1K views
- **Typical rates**: $0.20–$6 per 1K views (niche/platform dependent)
- **Multiplier**: Cross-post same clip to TikTok + Reels + Shorts if campaign allows

## 7-Step Workflow

### Step 1: Niche Selection
**Output**: Niche name, 3-5 example creators, 10 video titles to clip
**Criteria**: You watch this content, active campaigns exist, you know "best moments"

### Step 2: Platform & Campaign Selection
**Platforms** (free to join):
- Whop (clipping campaigns)
- Vyro (MrBeast-backed)
- Content Rewards
- ClipAffiliates / Clipping.net / Duzlo

**Campaign Checklist**:
- Rate ≥ $1/1K views (ideal)
- Per-clip cap ≥ $100
- Min view threshold < 20K
- Clear rules on sources/formats

### Step 3: Account Setup
- TikTok (primary) + optionally Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts
- Niche-specific username, 1-2 line bio, simple profile pic

### Step 4: Source Video Selection
**Criteria**: Strong emotional moments, clear 15-45s segments, good audio, campaign-allowed
**Output per video**: Source URL, 3-5 timestamps with descriptions

### Step 5: Clip Editing (CapCut example)
**Specs**: 9:16 (1080×1920), 15-45s, hook in first 1-2s, synced captions, campaign branding
**Process**: Import → Cut → Crop 9:16 → Auto-captions (fix errors) → Zooms/cuts → Trending audio (if allowed) → Export 1080p no watermark

### Step 6: Posting & Tracking
- Post 2-3 clips/day on TikTok (consistent schedule)
- Relevant hashtags + hook caption
- Submit post URLs to campaign dashboard immediately
- Log: URL, platform, date/time, topic, views at 24h/48h/7d

### Step 7: Optimization Loop (every 3-7 days)
- Identify top 10% by views, watch time, shares/saves
- Analyze patterns: topics, hooks, lengths, posting times
- Double down on winners, kill underperformers

## 30-Day Roadmap

### Week 1: Setup + First 10 Clips
- Pick niche, platform, campaign
- Create accounts
- Learn basic CapCut editing
- Post 10 clips (≈2/day)
- **Deliverables**: Niche doc, campaign brief, 10 posted clips logged

### Week 2: Volume + Data Collection
- 15-20 clips/week
- Test: hook styles, lengths (15s vs 30s vs 45s), topics
- Basic analysis of top performers
- **Deliverables**: 20+ new clips, first "what's working" notes

### Week 3: Systematize + Scale
- Create CapCut templates (caption style, colors, intro/outro)
- Use AI tools (OpusClip, Ssemble) for rough cuts
- 20-30 clips/week
- Cross-post to Reels/Shorts if allowed
- **Deliverables**: Template files, 20-30 clips, cross-platform plan

### Week 4: Optimize + First Payout Target
- Focus top 20% topics/hooks
- Maintain quality at volume
- Hit payout threshold if possible
- Document full SOP
- **Deliverables**: 20-30 clips, first withdrawal (if threshold met), written SOP

## Metrics to Track (per clip)
- clip_id, source_url, topic, hook_text, length_seconds, platform
- posted_at, views_24h, views_48h, views_7d
- likes, comments, shares, saves
- estimated_earnings (campaign CPM)

**Derived**: Avg views by topic/hook, best posting times, earnings per 1K vs campaign rate

## Risks & Rules (Hermes must remind)
- Only clip content explicitly allowed by campaign/license
- Follow platform + campaign TOS (no banned content, no misleading claims)
- Treat as skill-based side hustle with variance, not "get rich quick"
- Never pay to "unlock" campaigns - legitimate platforms are free to join
