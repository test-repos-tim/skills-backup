# Ikigai Business Finder

This skill provides comprehensive guidance for discovering profitable business ideas using the Ikigai framework, conducting market research, identifying unfair advantages, and building business offers.

## Key Features

- **7-Stage Process**: Ikigai discovery, niche sharpening, market research, unfair advantage, validation, offer building, and final summary
- **Comprehensive Coaching**: One-on-one business idea guidance with structured framework
- **Market Research Integration**: Competitive analysis and opportunity identification
- **Action Planning**: Complete business plans with implementation steps

## How It Works

The skill follows a structured 7-stage conversation:

1. **Ikigai Discovery**: Self-assessment to find business concept intersection
2. **Niche Sharpening**: Transform vague ideas into specific, marketable niches
3. **Market Research**: Web-based competitive analysis and gap identification
4. **Unfair Advantage**: Connect personal background to market opportunities
5. **Validation Check**: Quick assessment of business fundamentals and viability
6. **Offer + Pricing**: Build complete business offer with pricing strategy
7. **Final Summary**: Deliver comprehensive business plan with action steps

## When to Use

Use this skill when people need help with:
- Finding business ideas using the Ikigai framework
- Understanding what to sell or create
- Identifying their unique business niche
- Discovering their unfair advantage
- Building their first business offer
- Validating business concepts before investing time/money
