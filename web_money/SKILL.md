# Web Money Skill (Ireland, No‑Car)

## Overview
When you have no car and are based in Ireland, the fastest cash comes from remote micro‑task platforms and short‑term freelance work that can be done entirely online. The skill focuses on three proven paths:

1. Amazon Mechanical Turk (MTurk) – immediate task‑based pay.
2. Nielsen Computer & Mobile Panel – passive background earnings.
3. Free certifications (HubSpot Academy, freeCodeCamp) → quick freelance offers.

All three require only a laptop/phone, internet connection, and a bank account or PayPal for payouts.

## Path 1 – Amazon Mechanical Turk (MTurk)

**What you do:** Complete Human Intelligence Tasks (HITs) such as image labeling, short audio transcription, surveys, data verification, and simple categorization.

**Typical pay:** $0.05 – $2.00 per task; most tasks fall in the $0.10 – $0.50 range.

**How to start:**
1. Sign up at worker.mturk.com (approval usually within 24‑48 h).
2. Set up direct deposit to an Irish bank or use Amazon Payments balance.
3. Use a script like HIT Scraper or TurkOpticon to filter higher‑pay HITs (≥ $0.30).
4. Aim for 10‑20 tasks/hour → $1‑$10/hour depending on selection.

**First cash:** You can request payout once your balance hits $10 (Amazon gift card) or $10 via direct deposit (ACH). Expect first payout within 3‑5 days of starting.

**Realistic weekly earnings:** 10 h/week × $3/hour ≈ €28; 20 h/week ≈ €55.

**Scaling:** After 2‑4 weeks you learn which requesters pay well and can hit €100‑€150/mo part‑time.

## Path 2 – Nielsen Computer & Mobile Panel

**What you do:** Install the Nielsen app on your phone/PC; it anonymously tracks device usage (no personal data).

**Typical pay:** $5‑$10 per month per device (paid via gift card or PayPal).

**How to start:**
1. Apply at nationalsurveys.com/nielsen (acceptance is usually immediate for Irish residents).
2. Install the app on your primary phone and/or computer.
3. Leave it running; earnings accrue automatically.

**First cash:** First payout after ~30 days (monthly cycle).

**Realistic earnings:** €5‑€10/mo per device; with phone + laptop ≈ €10‑€20/mo.

## Path 3 – Free Certifications → Freelance

**What you do:** Complete a genuinely free, employer‑recognized certification, then offer the skill on freelance marketplaces (Fiverr, Upwork, PeoplePerHour) or directly to local Irish businesses.

**Best free certs (Ireland‑friendly):**

| Cert | Time | Employer Value |
|------|------|----------------|
| HubSpot Inbound Marketing | 3-8 h | High in marketing |
| HubSpot Content Marketing | 3-8 h | Medium |
| HubSpot SEO | 3-8 h | Medium |
| freeCodeCamp Web Dev | 300‑h series | Portfolio proof |
| IBM AI Engineering (Coursera) | 3‑mo (financial aid) | Tech roles |

**How to start (HubSpot path):**
1. Go to academy.hubspot.com → Inbound Marketing.
2. Complete all lessons + quiz; download badge.
3. Build a one-page portfolio: “HubSpot Inbound Marketing certified – SEO, blogging, email workflows.”
4. Create Fiverr gig: “HubSpot SEO audit / 1 blog outline” at €15.
5. Pitch 5 Irish SMEs via LinkedIn offering a free 15-min call.

**First cash:** Certification in 1 day; first gig €15‑€30 by week 2.
